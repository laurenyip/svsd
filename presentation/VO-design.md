# Voiceover: the design section (2:47–4:08)

Read at a relaxed pace, about 2.5 words a second. `/` is a short breath, `//` a full pause while the picture changes.
**Bold** words carry the stress. The times are where each line starts in the draft cut.

---

**2:47 · Images: the many directions we explored**

> AI served as an **accelerator**, / for research, / and for exploring different directions. //
> But **human judgement** was always needed. //
> It showed us **many** directions, / and **we** judged which ones actually worked in practice.

**3:01 · Particle stars, glowing orbs, glass sliders, dark theme**

> Claude generated a lot of options, **quickly**: / particle stars, / glowing orbs, / glass sliders, / dark and light themes. //
> We asked whether each one would actually work / for a **five-year-old** with a **cochlear implant**.

**3:14 · Before: particle star → After: the slider**

> The particle star was **beautiful**, / but children couldn't tell **which way** to change their sound. //
> So we moved to a **simple slider** / between the two sounds.

**3:26 · Before: glass knob → After: high-contrast ring**

> The glass knob looked nice, / but it was **hard to see**, / so we made it a plain, **high-contrast** ring.

**3:34 · Before: dark mode → After: a bright storybook**

> Dark mode looked polished, / but a **bright storybook** felt friendlier for young children.

**3:40 · One-direction pages, the dotted target zone, who's about to appear**

> We didn't just take the suggestions as they were. //
> We added **our own** ideas: / one-direction pages, / a dotted target zone, / and a silhouette of who's about to appear.

**3:52 · Before → after: the characters**

> And we **redrew the characters** ourselves. //
> AI gave us **breadth**; / our **judgement** gave us the right answer, / and pushed the quality **higher**.

**4:02 · End card** *(say it if you're closing the video, or hand back to Riya)*

> Thank you for watching, / and thank you for hosting the **SVSD** design competition.

---

**Recording tips**
- Record each block as its own take, with two seconds of silence before and after, so it's easy to slide onto the picture.
- Smile on the last line of each block; it lifts the ending.
- If a block runs long, cut the words in the list before cutting the "why" sentence. The reasoning is what judges score.
