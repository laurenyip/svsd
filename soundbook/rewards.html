Kids feel"I got a new one!" Each sticker is drawn from the shape of their own voice, so no two kids' books look the same.
Good forThe standard in kids' learning apps. Easy to grasp at 4, filling a page gives clear set completion, and it scales to any number of sounds.
Watch outCollections can feel flat over weeks: sticker 40 feels like sticker 4. Empty slots for future books can read as "not done yet".

Kids feel"My island is getting bigger." The friends and places from each story move in, so practice turns into a place they own and want to visit.
Good forLong-term motivation, like the meta-progression in Animal Crossing or Toca Boca. Progress is visible at a glance, and nothing is ever taken away.
Watch outThe most art and build work: every story needs pieces that fit the world. A world that only grows by finishing stories can stall a child who's stuck.

Kids feel"I did a big thing." One trophy per finished book plus medals for milestones makes a proud, show-a-grown-up moment.
Good forRare, big milestones, like game achievements. Medals double as a record the speech therapist can read (first SSS, 10 holds).
Watch outTrophies and medals carry winning and ranking. Gold, silver and bronze would grade the child, which breaks "warmer, not wrong". Only works with one trophy per book, never tiers.
